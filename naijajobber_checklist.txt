Startup Checklist
1. Foundation
Create: Business Plan, Financial Model (budget, cash flow, projections), Grant Budget Narrative,
Pitch Deck.
2. Company Documents
Company Profile, Mission & Vision, Product Overview, Org Chart, Hiring Plan, Partnership Strategy,
Brand Guidelines, Executive Summary.
3. Finance
3-year forecast, department budgets, burn rate, runway, revenue model, use of funds, grant
spending tracker, monthly P&L;, balance sheet, cash flow.
4. Product
PRD, technical architecture, roadmap, MVP scope, user journey, security/privacy plan.
5. Impact
KPIs: users, employers, jobs posted, placements, countries reached, revenue, community impact.
6. Operations
Separate accounting categories, asset register, compliance, monthly reporting, document
repository.
7. Master Folder
Business Plan, Company Profile, Pitch Deck, Financial Model, Grant Budget, Budget Narrative,
Revenue Model, Product Roadmap, PRD, Team Structure, Market Research, Impact Metrics, Risk
Assessment, Legal Docs
