1. Project Summary

NaijaJobber requests $250,000 over 12 months to accelerate an AI-enabled remote employment platform that connects African job seekers with verified global employers. The project directly supports youth and women entering remote work, improves employer trust through verification and moderation, and produces auditable impact metrics for funder reporting.

Grant amount
$250k
Duration
12 mo
Primary KPI
25 hires

2. Program Objectives

  1. Onboard 500+ verified job seeker profiles with CV, skills, and learning pathways.
  2. Recruit 50 verified employers offering remote-friendly roles.
  3. Publish 100+ quality job listings with structured application data.
  4. Document 25 successful placements with hire confirmation workflow.
  5. Launch grant-ready impact dashboard (users, jobs, countries, demographics).

6. Impact Measurement & Reporting

NaijaJobber will report quarterly using platform-native metrics:

  • Registered and verified job seekers (with optional gender/youth tags where consented)
  • Verified employers and active job posts (remote vs hybrid)
  • Applications submitted and interviews scheduled
  • Confirmed hires (employer attestation + seeker confirmation)
  • Countries and cities reached
  • Learning module completions and referral participation
Exports available in CSV/JSON from the admin impact module for funder dashboards. All personal data reporting is aggregated and anonymized per privacy policy.

7. Sustainability Beyond Grant

Employer subscription revenue (Basic/Pro/Enterprise) and featured job fees are projected to cover ~60% of operating costs by month 18. The platform architecture is designed for long-term operation without grant dependency; this funding de-risks launch and establishes marketplace liquidity.
